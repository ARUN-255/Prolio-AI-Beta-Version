# Prolio-ai
An advanced AI-powered portfolio and recruitment platform where studentscan build resumes, generate portfolio websites and create AI chatbots from thier profile -- while recruiters can search , compare , and hire candidates using intelligent tools - built for students , designed for recruiters.
## For Students 
-Build ATS-friendly resumes with 25+ templates
-Generate a full portfolio website with a shareable link
-Create an AI chatbot that answers questions about you
-Check your resumes ATS score and get improvement tips
-Upload certificates and achievements
-Get AI-generated blog posts from your projects
-Discover new project ideas based on your skills
-Ask coding and clear doubts to an AI assistant
## For Recruiters/HR
-Search and filter candidates by skill , location and exprience
-Compare multiple resumes using AI
-Get a 60-ssecond AI summary of any candidate
-Paste a job description - AI finds the best matching resumes
-Click any projects - AI explains it in depth
-Post j0b listing for students to appy
